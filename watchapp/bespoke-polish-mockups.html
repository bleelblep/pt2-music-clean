dreamwave — bespoke polish mockups

Three problems, three groups of fixes. 1. Secondary text ships as GColorDarkGray (#555) at 14px — washed out on Emery's 64-color e-paper. 2. The Now Playing More popup (big 28px action name, value underneath, circular icon row) is a design language used nowhere else, so it reads as a foreign screen. 3. The bespoke Home is a status card over four bare rows and earns neither its space nor its place. Rendered at real Emery pixels (200×228), scaled 2×; coordinates map directly onto the draw_* calls in main.c. Fonts approximate Pebble Gothic — weights and positions are exact, glyph shapes are not. Cards marked recommended are my picks.

Update — unification pass: the last three sections cover the consistency plan. The shared grid (S1) gives lists and pagers one set of furniture coordinates (eyebrow (18,18), footer (8,202)). Input Choice (S3) becomes the third pager. Searching / Buffering / Error (S4) drop the last accent header bands for the chromeless language.

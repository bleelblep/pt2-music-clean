What these renders are and aren't

Colors here are the exact bespoke_colors() hex values, but a browser shows them on a backlit panel — the Time 2's reflective LCD will read duller and flatter, and thin accent strokes lose more than solid fills do. Type is a stand-in: the watch draws Pebble's Gothic at fixed sizes, so line breaks and the exact width of a title will move.

Treat these as layout and hierarchy, not as color proofs. Anything that survives the switcher across all six themes here will survive on the watch; anything that only works in one is worth a second look.
